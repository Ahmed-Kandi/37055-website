Rookie Year

Starting from zero

This is our first season in FIRST Tech Challenge. No hand-me-down robot, no playbook from past years — just a group of students who wanted to build something. Every mechanism, every line of code, and every connection we make with our community is a first for us.

Being a rookie team means we get to set our own culture: learn fast, share what we learn, and practice Gracious Professionalism® with every team we meet.

Our Subteams

Every member belongs to a subteam, and every subteam depends on the others to get a robot on the field.

💻

Programming

Our programmers write the robot's code in Java using SolversLib, a command-based FTC library that keeps our code organized and reusable.

  • Command-based TeleOp & Autonomous
  • Subsystems for drivetrain and mechanisms
  • Sensors, odometry & path following
🔧

CAD & Build

From first sketch to competition-ready hardware: this subteam designs the robot in CAD, then cuts, prints, and assembles it.

  • 3D modeling & design reviews
  • Prototyping and 3D printing
  • Drivetrain, wiring & mechanism assembly
🌎

Outreach

Outreach connects our team with the community — sharing STEM, finding sponsors, and helping other teams along the way.

  • STEM demos & community events
  • Sponsorships & fundraising
  • Social media & engineering portfolio
