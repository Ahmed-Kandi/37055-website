About

Our Rookie Season

This is our first year competing in FIRST Tech Challenge. We are starting without a previous robot or code base, so everything on this year's robot is designed and built by the current team.

As a rookie team, our goals for the season are to field a reliable robot, document what we learn in our engineering portfolio, and build connections with other teams and our community.

Subteams

How the Team Is Organized

Each member works on one of three subteams.

Programming

Writes the robot's TeleOp and Autonomous code in Java using SolversLib, a command-based library for FTC.

  • Command-based structure with subsystems
  • Driver controls and autonomous routines
  • Sensors, odometry, and path following

CAD & Build

Designs the robot in CAD, then fabricates and assembles it.

  • 3D modeling and design reviews
  • Prototyping and 3D printing
  • Drivetrain, mechanisms, and wiring

Outreach

Represents the team in the community and manages our public presence.

  • STEM events and demonstrations
  • Sponsorships and fundraising
  • Social media and the engineering portfolio
